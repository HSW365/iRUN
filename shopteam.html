QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Do I need to give iRun my Shopify login?

No. iRun reads your products from your store's public product feed, the same information any shopper can see. You never install an app or share a password with it.

What do I connect?

Your Instagram, from your live page, so the team can post for you. It needs to be a Business or Creator account. TikTok is optional. Until a platform is connected, ads wait instead of posting.

Will it make claims about my products?

No. Every fact in an ad comes from your own product page: the title, price, photos and the details you wrote. It does not invent reviews, results or discounts.

Which product does it advertise each day?

New products first, then it rotates through your whole live catalog. Products that are sold out or have no photo are skipped. Once every product has had a turn, it starts a new lap with new scenes.

What does the QR do?

It opens your store's checkout with that exact product already in the cart. Shoppers scan from another phone or tap it in the caption link. One step instead of five.

Does iRun take a cut of my sales?

No. It's a flat $27 a month. Every sale goes through your own Shopify checkout to you.

Can I stop it?

Yes. Pause or cancel from your live page whenever you want. Nothing to sign.
