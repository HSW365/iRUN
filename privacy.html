Who we are

iRun is a social media management service run by HSW365 Media LLC ("we", "us"), based in New Jersey, USA. This policy explains what we collect when you use iRun at hsw365.github.io/iRUN, including when you connect an Instagram account, and what we do with it. Questions: hsw365media@gmail.com.

Instagram data

When you connect Instagram, you sign in on Instagram and approve the permissions below. iRun never sees your Instagram password.

PermissionWhat iRun uses it for
instagram_business_basicShow your username, profile photo, follower counts and recent posts in your iRun dashboard.
instagram_business_content_publishPublish photos and reels to your account, only when you press Publish or schedule a post.
instagram_business_manage_commentsShow comments on your posts and let you reply to, hide or delete them.
instagram_business_manage_messagesShow DMs people send you and let you reply. iRun only replies inside conversations the other person started, within Instagram's 24-hour window. It never sends bulk or unsolicited messages.

We store your Instagram user ID, username, display name, profile photo URL, account type and an access token, so iRun can act for you while you stay connected. Posts, comments and messages are read from Instagram when you open them and are not kept by iRun, except media you upload to publish, which is hosted so Instagram can fetch it.

Other information

  • Account details you give us when you sign up for an iRun plan: business name, email, website and social handles.
  • Payment records: plan, amount and status. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Content iRun makes for you, such as scripts, captions and videos, and a log of what was posted.
  • Leads: if someone comments or DMs one of your keywords, we log their username and message so you can follow up.

How we use it

Only to run iRun for you: show your dashboard, publish what you approve, manage comments and replies, send your reports, and provide support. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services that run iRun (Supabase for hosting and storage, Meta for Instagram, Stripe for card payments, and our AI providers to draft captions and replies). Each only gets what it needs to do that job.

Storage and security

Data is stored with Supabase in the United States. Access tokens are kept on our servers only and never sent to your browser. Instagram tokens expire after 60 days unless renewed while you stay connected.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you are connected or subscribed. When you disconnect Instagram, your token and stored Instagram profile data are deleted right away. Billing records are kept as long as the law requires.

Deleting your data

  • In iRun: open Instagram, Settings and press Disconnect Instagram.
  • On Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, remove iRun. Instagram tells us and we delete your data automatically.
  • By email: write to hsw365media@gmail.com from the address on your account. We delete within 30 days and confirm by email.

See data deletion for details.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. California and other US state residents have these rights under their state laws, and we honor them for everyone.

Children

iRun is for businesses and creators aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for significant changes, email active customers.
