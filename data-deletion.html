What gets deleted

Your Instagram access token, your stored Instagram profile details (user ID, username, name, profile photo URL, account type) and the link between your Instagram account and iRun. After that, iRun can no longer read or post to your account.

How to delete your data

  • In iRun: open Instagram, Settings and press Disconnect Instagram. Deletion is immediate.
  • On Instagram: go to Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, and remove iRun. Instagram notifies us and we delete your data automatically. You get a confirmation code you can check on this page.
  • By email: write to hsw365media@gmail.com from the address on your account and say "Delete my data". We delete within 30 days and confirm by email.

Check a request

If you removed iRun on Instagram, you received a confirmation code. Open this page with that code, or enter it below.
