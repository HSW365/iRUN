Five jobs. One loop.

A fundraise is a pipeline, not a pile of tabs. iRun works it every hour and shows you what moved.

01Perfect the deckUpload the PDF. Get a readiness score, what an investor will doubt, a fix for every slide and a rewritten one-liner and pitch.
02Match investorsBring your list. Each investor is scored 0 to 100 against your stage, check size, sector and region, with the reasons next to the score.
03Draft the emailA short personal first email for every strong match, written only from your deck, your traction and what you know about them.
04Send what you approveRead it, edit it, approve it. It goes from your sending address with an unsubscribe link, plus one follow-up after four days.
05Book the meetingReplies are caught, follow-ups stop, and every investor moves across one pipeline from matched to meeting.

Built so it cannot embarrass you.

One bad email to an investor costs more than a month of the tool. So the rules are hard.

Facts

It never invents traction

Emails and rewrites use only your deck, your notes and what the investor row says. No made-up revenue, customers, logos or deadlines. If a fact is missing it tells you to add it.

Control

Nothing sends without your OK

Every email waits for you. Approve one, approve all 70+, edit the wording or pass. Sending is off until you switch it on, and capped at 15 a day.

Compliance

Clean outreach only

Every email carries an unsubscribe link and a postal address. Unsubscribes and bounces are never emailed again. You bring the list, so you only contact people you are allowed to.

One plan. The whole fundraise.

Deck doctor, matching, drafting, outreach, reply tracking and the meetings pipeline. Credits cover the work: a deck review is 1 credit, 10 investor emails are 1 credit, and extra credits are 50 cents.
