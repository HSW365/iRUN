Five agents. One business.

Each one does a single job and writes down what it did, so you can watch the whole company run from one page.

  • ONBOARD
    Reads your businessYour website and the notes you give it become a profile: what you sell, who buys it, how you talk, and the facts it is allowed to use. Nothing invented.
  • WEBSITE
    Builds your siteA fast one-page site designed from your profile. You see the draft first; it goes live when you approve it.
  • CONTENT
    Posts every dayShort videos with script, voiceover and captions, plus a daily faceless story video on Pro and Elite, posted to your TikTok and Instagram.
  • OUTREACH
    Emails your prospectsA personal first email and one follow-up to the business contacts you upload. Unsubscribes are honored instantly; replies stop the follow-up.
  • CEO
    Reviews every nightReads the day's results and its own memory, writes new angles, sets tomorrow's plan, and emails you a short report with anything only you can do.
